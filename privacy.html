SohailSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

SohailSnap ("the app") is a Windows screenshot tool. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your screenshots go only to your own Google Drive, and we do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Screen contents are captured only when you press the screenshot key or click the tray icon. Screenshots stay on your computer unless you choose to upload, copy or save them.
  • Google account / Google Drive: when you click Upload, the app asks you to sign in with Google and requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. With it, the app can only create and access the files it uploads itself (in a folder named "SohailSnap Screenshots"). It cannot read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.

2. How the data is used

The app uses Google Drive only to upload the screenshots you choose to upload, to set their sharing to "anyone with the link can view", and to give you the link. Nothing else is done with your Google account.

3. Storage and security

  • Screenshots are stored only in your own Google Drive account. The developer has no access to them.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only on your computer (in %AppData%\SohailSnap) and encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI). It is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Settings and a list of your recent links are kept only on your computer.
  • The app has no servers, analytics, advertising or tracking.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any user data to anyone. A screenshot's link is visible only to the people you choose to send it to.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

SohailSnap's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your choices

  • Delete uploaded screenshots any time in Google Drive.
  • Remove the app's access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or use "Sign out of Google Drive" in the app's tray menu.
  • Delete the folder %AppData%\SohailSnap to remove all local settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
