SohailSnap

SohailSnap is a free screenshot tool for Windows. Press a key, select an area of your screen, mark it up, and share it with one click.

⬇ Download SohailSnap for Windows
Version 1.4 · Windows 10 / 11 · free · nothing to install

New in 1.4
  • Compare two screenshots in one image — take the 1st area, switch to another program (or stay on the same screen), take the 2nd, and SohailSnap joins them side by side or one above the other, ready to mark up and share.
  • Clearer crosshair — a large white crosshair with a black outline, easy to see on any background.

Install in 1 minute

  1. Right-click the downloaded SohailSnap-Windows.zip → Properties → tick Unblock → OK (avoids the "Windows protected your PC" warning).
  2. Extract it to a folder you keep, e.g. Documents\SohailSnap, and double-click SohailSnap.exe.
  3. Press Print Screen, select an area, click the cloud button. The first time, sign in with Google (click Continue on the "app not verified" page).
  4. Optional: right-click the tray icon → tick Start with Windows.

Updating from an older version? Right-click the tray icon → Exit, replace SohailSnap.exe with the new one and start it again. Your sign-in and settings are kept.

Features

  • Select an area, a full screen, the last area again, or take a delayed screenshot
  • Compare two areas — even from two different programs — in one image
  • Pen, line, arrow, rectangle, highlighter, text, blur and numbered-step tools
  • Magnifier with color codes
  • Upload to your own Google Drive and get a full-quality share link copied to your clipboard
  • Copy to clipboard or save as PNG / JPG
  • Every keyboard shortcut can be changed

How Google Drive is used

SohailSnap asks for the limited Google permission drive.file. It can only create and see the screenshot files it uploads for you, in a folder named "SohailSnap Screenshots" in your own Drive. It cannot see any of your other files. Uploaded screenshots are shared as "anyone with the link can view" so the link works for the people you send it to. You can delete them or change sharing at any time in Google Drive.
