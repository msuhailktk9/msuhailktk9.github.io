SohailSnap

SohailSnap is a free screenshot tool for Windows. Press a key, select an area of your screen, mark it up, and share it with one click.

Features

  • Select an area, a full screen, the last area again, or take a delayed screenshot
  • Pen, line, arrow, rectangle, highlighter, text, blur and numbered-step tools
  • Magnifier with color codes
  • Upload to your own Google Drive and get a share link copied to your clipboard
  • Copy to clipboard or save as PNG / JPG
  • Every keyboard shortcut can be changed

How Google Drive is used

SohailSnap asks for the limited Google permission drive.file. It can only create and see the screenshot files it uploads for you, in a folder named "SohailSnap Screenshots" in your own Drive. It cannot see any of your other files. Uploaded screenshots are shared as "anyone with the link can view" so the link works for the people you send it to. You can delete them or change sharing at any time in Google Drive.
